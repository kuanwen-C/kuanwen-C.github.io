About me

I'm Steven, a master's student in Software Engineering at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science. I like making systems faster, cheaper and easier to operate.

As an undergraduate at National Cheng Kung University, I studied both software and hardware circuit courses: operating systems, computer networks, database systems and algorithms on one side, microprocessors and circuits on the other. That mix is why I'm comfortable anywhere from assembly on a microcontroller to pipelines in the cloud.

Since then I've done two internships. At Tomofun, the company behind the Furbo pet camera, I worked on MLOps and cloud infrastructure, and both of my projects were about cost. I moved the object-detection model from GPUs to a cheaper AWS inference accelerator and restructured the pipeline around it, which cut total inference processing time by over 50%. I also redesigned the AWS pipeline that collects inference logs, which cut daily log storage by nearly 90% (from about 180 GB to 20 GB) and made the data queryable with SQL.

At GliaCloud, a video advertising company, I worked as a data scientist. I built the Django ETL pipelines that bring Google Ad Manager data into the team's dashboards, then built on that data: a statistical model that spots unusual traffic and alerts engineers on Slack, a mathematical model that forecasts monthly revenue deductions, and a click-through-rate model with an LLM assistant that suggests new video ad concepts.

I'm now looking for a Summer 2027 software engineering internship focused on infrastructure and data pipelines.

Experience

AI Engineer Intern — MLOps

Tomofun (Furbo) · Taipei, Taiwan

Jul 2025 – Mar 2026
01 · Inference acceleration

Half the processing time, on cheaper hardware

Problem
A legacy C/Darknet YOLOv4 pipeline ran on GPUs and inference was expensive.
Approach
Ported it to PyTorch and split it in two. The convolutional graph has fixed tensor shapes, so I compiled it with the AWS Neuron SDK for Inferentia2. Decoding and non-maximum suppression are dynamic, so I rewrote them to match the original model and ran them on parallel CPU threads on a separate instance. The accelerator only does convolution.
Result
Over 50% less total processing time than the original GPU pipeline, including pre- and post-processing.

PyTorch · AWS Neuron SDK · Inferentia2 · Python · C

02 · Cloud infrastructure

A log pipeline that splits fast monitoring from heavy analytics

Problem
Log volume was growing, and monitoring and analytics were coupled and hard to maintain.
Approach
Redesigned and rebuilt the pipeline on AWS. Kinesis collects logs across accounts and feeds two independent paths. For analytics, Lambda filters and normalizes records, which are stored as Parquet and organized by date and log type. Everything is defined in Python with AWS CDK, with CI/CD to preview and deploy changes, plus smoke and integration tests.
Result
Daily storage fell from ~180 GB to 20 GB, and the data became queryable with SQL.

AWS CDK · Kinesis · Firehose · Lambda · S3 · Glue · Athena · CloudWatch
